# Product Guidelines

## Tone of Voice
The game should adopt a **humorous and lighthearted** tone. This approach aims for broad appeal and helps to alleviate potential frustration, especially when players attempt to "hack" the LLM judge. The humor should be engaging and playful, even in challenging in-game situations.

## Visual Identity
The visual identity for "Data and Dragons" should be **modern and clean**. This aesthetic will feature sleek, minimalist design with clear UI elements, potentially incorporating motifs inspired by data visualization. For the initial version, the interface will be primarily textual, with the "modern and clean" principle guiding its presentation and readability.

## Brand Messaging
The brand messaging for "Data and Dragons" will emphasize being **accessible and fun**. This highlights the game's entertainment value and aims for ease of entry for a wide audience. While showcasing innovative LLM and agentic solutions, the primary focus will be on the engaging and enjoyable experience for the player.
